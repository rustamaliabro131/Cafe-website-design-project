Our Heritage & Craft

Founded on the simple philosophy that great coffee nurtures meaningful conversations (GupShup), we source directly from family-owned micro-lots in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Sumatra.

Every bean is profiled by roast masters to highlight its natural floral, chocolate, and citrus aromatics without harsh bitterness. Combined with locally-sourced organic milks and pure botanicals, we craft drinks designed to elevate every moment of your day.
